### Day 2 — Educational tip

**Post**

A pricing rule worth keeping: your hourly rate is not your profit.

Fuel, travel time, helper pay, consumables, callbacks, admin, and overhead all come out before profit. A quote that only multiplies hours by a rate can look busy while quietly losing money.
